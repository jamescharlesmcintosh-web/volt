01 The Pass Method

Every line is a decision. Nothing here is accidental.

A haircut is a sequence of about four hundred small choices. Angle of the guard. Pressure of the thumb. Where the weight line breaks. I make them in silence, at speed, and I do not get a second take.

The clipper does not negotiate. It either leaves a clean gradient or it does not. That standard is the whole business.

— Marcus Hayes
